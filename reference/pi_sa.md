# Production in industry - Seasonally and calendar adjusted

A multivariate time series object containing the volume index of
production (Index, 2015=100) for mining and quarrying; manufacturing;
electricity, gas, steam and air conditioning supply for the EU-28
countries and EU-28 and EA-19 (Euro Area) aggregates.

## Usage

``` r
data(pi_sa)
```

## Format

A multivariate time series objects with 30 time series of length 234.
